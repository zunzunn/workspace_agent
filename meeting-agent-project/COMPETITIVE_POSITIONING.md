# Competitive Positioning

## Category

AI-powered meeting and team operations.

## Existing baseline

A user can combine:
- Google Calendar
- Google Meet
- Gmail
- ChatGPT or another general AI assistant
- task tools
- team chat

The product must therefore provide value from **orchestration and context**, not simply from adding a chat box.

## Product differentiation hypothesis

The product combines:
1. organizational context
2. calendar state
3. meeting lifecycle
4. controlled autonomous actions
5. approval and verification
6. persistent preferences
7. a team workspace

## Positioning statement

> Meeting Agent is an AI operating layer for small teams that turns meeting requests into verified actions and carries meeting work from scheduling through follow-up.

This is a hypothesis, not a proven market claim.
