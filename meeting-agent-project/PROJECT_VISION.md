# Project Vision

## Vision

Build an AI meeting operating layer for small teams that understands people, calendars, meetings, context, and follow-up work.

## Product thesis

Meetings are not isolated calendar events. They are work units with a lifecycle:

1. Before — decide why the meeting exists and prepare.
2. Schedule — find the right people and time.
3. During — understand what happens.
4. After — turn decisions into actions.
5. Follow-up — ensure commitments do not disappear.

The product should connect these stages.

## Long-term vision

A user should be able to express an outcome in natural language:

> "I need everyone working on Project X to meet tomorrow. Find a time, schedule it, prepare an agenda based on our previous discussions, and leave a 15-minute buffer around my other meetings."

The system should:
- understand intent
- identify relevant people
- inspect authorized calendars
- consider constraints and preferences
- generate candidate plans
- surface ambiguity
- request approval when required
- execute approved actions
- preserve an auditable action history
- prepare the meeting
- help with follow-up

## What success feels like

The user should feel:

> "I told my meeting agent what outcome I need, and it handled the coordination without making me babysit the calendar."

## What this product is not

- Not simply a calendar clone.
- Not merely a chatbot connected to Calendar.
- Not a generic autonomous agent with unrestricted permissions.
- Not a replacement for every productivity tool on day one.
