# User Personas

## Primary persona: Small-business team member

Needs:
- schedule meetings quickly
- avoid conflicts
- understand today's agenda
- prepare for important meetings
- keep track of decisions
- coordinate with colleagues

Pain points:
- calendar overload
- repeated scheduling conversations
- context scattered across tools
- forgotten follow-ups
- unclear meeting purpose

## Secondary persona: Team coordinator / founder / manager

Needs:
- coordinate multiple people
- protect focus time
- understand team scheduling constraints
- reduce unnecessary meetings
- maintain recurring meeting hygiene
- keep projects moving after meetings

## Product principle

Do not assume every team member wants the same degree of automation. Preferences and permissions should be configurable.
